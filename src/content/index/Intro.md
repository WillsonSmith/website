Hey! I'm **Willson**, a developer who likes to help people do
neat things.

If you need a label to hang me on, start with *front-end* —
that's where I spent my career. But front-end was never the
whole story; it's just where I could most directly shape how software feels
to use. These days I work solo, which means I
build full-stack — wherever the experience needs me to be.

Previously, I worked at Shopify for 8 years. My final role: **Senior Creative Technologist**. In that role I shaped the merchant admin experience, contributed to the Polaris design system, and built prototypes that turned ideas into tangible products. I wore many hats with one through-line: building bridges between people and technology.
