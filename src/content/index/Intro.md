Hey! I'm **Willson**, a developer who likes to help people do
neat things.

If you need a label to hang me on, start with *front-end* —
that's where I spent my career. But front-end was never the
whole story; it's just where I could shape how software feels
to use, most directly. These days I work solo, which means I
build full-stack — wherever the experience needs me to be.

For 8 years I was a **Senior Creative Technologist** at
Shopify, helping shape the merchant admin, contributing to the
Polaris design system, and building prototypes that turned
ideas into something people could actually feel. Different
hats, one through-line: building bridges between people and
technology.
